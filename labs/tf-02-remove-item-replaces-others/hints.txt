Look closely at the `terraform plan` output: `service_token[2]` is marked for
destruction, and `worker_fingerprint` changes. Why did removing `"billing"` (the
middle item) affect `"worker"`?
---
`count` addresses resource instances by numeric index (`0, 1, 2`). Removing index `1`
shifts `"worker"` from `[2]` to `[1]`, so Terraform treats `[1]` as `"worker"` and
destroys `[2]`.
---
Migrate `random_password.service_token` from `count` to `for_each = toset(local.services)`,
add `moved` blocks from `[0]` -> `["api"]` and `[2]` -> `["worker"]`, then run
`terraform apply` so only `[1]` (`billing`) is destroyed.
