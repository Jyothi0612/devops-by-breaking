A Service does not know about Deployments. It finds its pods with a label selector, and
Kubernetes writes the matching pod IPs into the Service's endpoints. Look at the
endpoints first:

    kubectl -n shop get endpointslices
    kubectl -n shop describe svc web
---
Compare two things side by side: the selector on the Service, and the labels on the pods.

    kubectl -n shop get svc web -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl -n shop get pods --show-labels
---
The Service selects `app=web-frontend`. The pods are labelled `app=web`. One of the two
has to change. Which is the safer one to change: the label on running pods that a
Deployment owns, or the selector on the Service?
