Check `kubectl -n ops auth can-i list pods --as=system:serviceaccount:ops:pod-watcher`
and list the `Role` and `RoleBinding` objects in namespace `ops`.
---
`Role/pod-reader` exists in `ops`, but there is no `RoleBinding` linking it to
`ServiceAccount/pod-watcher`.
---
Create a `RoleBinding` in namespace `ops` binding `pod-reader` to `pod-watcher`
(do **not** bind `cluster-admin`):
`kubectl -n ops create rolebinding pod-watcher-read --role=pod-reader --serviceaccount=ops:pod-watcher`.
