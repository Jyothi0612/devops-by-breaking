`NetworkPolicy/default-deny-all` blocks both `Ingress` and `Egress` for all pods in
namespace `payments`. Deleting it would expose `api` to `rogue` pods.
---
`frontend` needs two `Egress` rules (DNS on UDP/TCP port 53 to `kube-system`, and
TCP port 80 to `app: api`), and `api` needs an `Ingress` rule allowing TCP port 80
from `app: frontend`.
---
Create `NetworkPolicy` objects in `payments` for `podSelector: {app: frontend}`
(egress to `kube-system` port 53 + `app: api` port 80) and `podSelector: {app: api}`
(ingress from `app: frontend` port 80), leaving `default-deny-all` in place.
