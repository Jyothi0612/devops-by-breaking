`docker run --rm dbb-sec-01:app ls /app` does not show `.env`, yet the scanner found
`DBB_DEMO_SECRET_NOT_REAL_01` inside `dbb-sec-01:app`. Inspect `docker history dbb-sec-01:app`.
---
Each `COPY` and `RUN` instruction in a `Dockerfile` creates an immutable layer. Deleting
`/app/.env` in a later `RUN` layer hides it from the final filesystem, but the earlier
`COPY . /app` layer still contains it.
---
Add `.env` to `.dockerignore` in `workspace/sec-01/` (and/or stop copying `.env`), then
rebuild `dbb-sec-01:app` with `./build.sh` so `.env` never enters any layer.
