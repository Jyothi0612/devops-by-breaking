`CrashLoopBackOff` means the container starts and exits non-zero. Check its logs with
`kubectl -n billing logs -l app=worker --previous`.
---
The container logs `FATAL: QUEUE_URL is not set`. Inspect `deploy/worker` to see
where its environment variables come from, then inspect that ConfigMap.
---
`ConfigMap/worker-config` has `QUEUE_URI` instead of `QUEUE_URL`. Update the key to
`QUEUE_URL` in `worker-config` and restart the `worker` pod (`kubectl -n billing
delete pods -l app=worker`).
